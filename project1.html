Project Summary

This project was during my Senior Project class. It is a turn based RPG with heavy inspiration from Slay The Spire. None of the art or music in this game was made by me. This game was fully done as a portfolio piece where I focused on showing off my coding expertise. The game was fully made in Godot and was the first project I made where I did a full dive into coding a game in Godot. I made this game basically in 2 months as I had a problem occur mid project where I lost almost all of my progress. The game is made to show off my coding capabilities, so all of the heavy work I did is within the code. The art is outsourced artwork from several different pages on Itch.io and the music is made by one person that I found through YouTube. More information can be found on my Itch.io page for the game where you can play the game here.

Gameplay

As mentioned before, the gameplay for this game is a turn based RPG that has heavy inspiration from Slay The Spire. The combat system is focused around a pool of 4 cards that do different things for the player to use. The attack card that lets the player choose 1 enemy to deal 10 damage to, the defense card that lets the player equip a shield that blocks up to 7 damage per defense card, the heal card that heals the player of 5 hit points, and the special card that makes the next 2 cards cost 0 mana and refreshes itself with a different card. How the block mechanic works, is that the shield takes up all the damage the number says it does, and when it runs out it still gates the damage from hitting the player's hp. So if the player recieves 15 damage but they have 1 shield, the shield breaks and the player takes no hp damage. Each card has a mana cost that is located at the top right of the card and the mana that the player is allowed to use during their turn is located at the top left of the screen. Each time it is the player's turn. their "hand" is filled with 7 randomly picked cards for them to use.

After the player uses all their mana, the turn is handed over to the enemies where each enemy attacks the player. After all the enemies take their turn attacking, the turn hands back over to the player and they recieve a new hand and a refreshed mana pool. The game functions around a "round" system, where every time the player defeats all the enemies, the round count goes up by 1, all the enemies respawn, and enemies deal 1 more damage for each round. It is an endless game where the player just tries to get as many points as possible. The player gets 1 score for every enemy killed, and the rounds passed are also tracked. The game ends when the player runs out of hp, where the game over screen appears with the player's score, rounds passed, and a home screen button. The game has more gameplay mechanics planned for the future, but I hope you enjoy the game as it is right now!
